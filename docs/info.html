About the services we offer

Our Prayer Schedule

We only have prayers every Sunday at 11:00 am.

When a Holiday fall on Monday-Saturday, we will have prayers at 10:30 am.

When a holiday falls on a Sunday, it will still be held at 11:00 am.

Sunday School

We have Children’s Sunday School for children in grades 1-6 every Sunday after services, from approx 12:15 pm to 2:15.

We have Teen Sunday School for all teens in grades 6-12 every Sunday from 2:30 pm to 4:30.

Price: $450/quarterly

There is no Sunday school when a holiday falls on a Sunday. A meal is included for both children and teens. $30/quarterly fee for students with allergies.
